## Tailoring

Sources: [Zockify leveling](https://www.zockify.com/forever/professions/tailoring/leveling/) (marked work in progress, "a few things are still based on the Classic client", updated 1 Oct 2026), [Kami-labs](https://kami-labs.fr/en/guide-couture-wow-forever/) (steps above 225 marked "after the beta"; only part of the page was read word for word), [Warcraft Tavern overview](https://www.warcrafttavern.com/forever/guides/tailoring/).

**Zockify route:** 1–45 60 Bolt of Linen Cloth · 45–55 10 Heavy Linen Gloves (only if you can disenchant) · 55–77 ~35 Bolt of Woolen Cloth · 80–85 6 Woolen Cape (lists Green Dye) · 85–95 12 Gray Woolen Shirt · 95–100 5 Double-stitched Woolen Shoulders · 100–120 40 Bolt of Silk Cloth · 120–135 17 Azure Silk Hood · 135–140 5 Formal White Shirt · 140–150 80 Bolt of Mageweave · 150–170 20 Crimson Silk Vest · 170–180 10 Crimson Silk Pantaloons · 180–190 12 Orange Mageweave Shirt · 190–215 38 Black Mageweave Gloves · (no step 215–220) · 220–245 ~27 Black Mageweave Gloves · 245–250 ~7 Black Mageweave Headband · 250–260 195 Bolt of Runecloth (4 Runecloth each) · 260–280 25 Runecloth Belt · 280–295 18 Runecloth Gloves · 295–300 5 Runecloth Headband. Several of these recipes are not learnable at those skills by the datamined learn skills (e.g. Bolt of Woolen Cloth 75, Gray Woolen Shirt 100, Bolt of Silk Cloth 125).

**Kami-labs route:** 1–26 Bolt of Linen Cloth · 26–75 Linen Belt · 75–110 Woolen Cape (Fine Thread) · 110–125 Double-stitched Woolen Shoulders · 125–138 Bolt of Silk Cloth · 138–149 Bright Yellow Shirt (Alliance vendor) · 149–157 Azure Silk Hood · 157–167 Dark Silk Shirt · 167–170 Silk Headband · 170–175 Formal White Shirt · 175–183 Bolt of Mageweave · 183–187 Red Swashbuckler's Shirt · 187–205 Crimson Silk Vest · 205–215 Black Mageweave Leggings · 215–223 Orange Mageweave Shirt · 223–229 Black Mageweave Leggings · then (after the beta) Black Mageweave Gloves, Simple Black Dress, Black Mageweave Headband, Bolt of Runecloth, Shadoweave Mask, Runecloth Belt, Cloak, Gloves, Boots. Merchant total "about 42 gold 18 silver".

**Forever changes:** about 180 new recipes; Red Linen Bag 8 slots; reagent bags (Linen 6, Woolen 8, Silk 10, Mageweave 12, Runecloth 14, Mooncloth 18, Bottomless 20 slots); camp recipes Faction Banner (20), Spinning Wheel (140, drops from Unstable Sentinel in City of Dalaran), Loom (300); a Legacy point at 150, 225 and 300; Tailoring Certification for 1,000 Merchant's Favor at 300; Craftsman's Writs give Durotar Supply and Logistics reputation from Journeyman (75), Expert (125) and Artisan (200).

## Enchanting

Sources: [Zockify leveling](https://www.zockify.com/forever/professions/enchanting/leveling/) (updated 9 Oct 2026, "a few things are still based on the Classic client"), [Zockify trainers](https://www.zockify.com/forever/professions/enchanting/trainers/), [SeeMeta](https://seemeta.com/en/wow-forever/professions/enchanting) (17 Oct 2026, 1.60.1 beta; partly summarised by the fetch tool), [Warcraft Tavern overview](https://www.warcrafttavern.com/forever/guides/enchanting/).

**Zockify route:** 1–2 Runed Copper Rod · 2–20 18 Enchant Bracer – Inferior Stamina (Mote of Magic, 10c at vendors) · 20–65 50 Lesser Magic Wand · 65–70 5 more · 70–150 Greater Magic Wand, or: 70–85 Chest – Inferior Stamina, 85–100 Bracer – Minor Stamina, 100–101 Runed Silver Rod, 101–110 Bracer – Minor Stamina, 110–135 Cloak – Minor Agility (Kulwia, Stonetalon), 130–150 Bracer – Lesser Stamina · 150–151 Runed Golden Rod · 151–165 Bracer – Lesser Strength (Kulwia) · 165–185 Boots – Lesser Agility · 185–200 Bracer – Strength · 200–201 Runed Truesilver Rod · 201–220 Bracer – Strength · 220–225 Cloak – Greater Defense · 225–235 Gloves – Agility · 235–250 Chest – Superior Stamina · 250–260 Bracer – Greater Strength · 260–265 Bracer – Greater Intellect · 265–285 Shield – Greater Stamina (Daniel Bartlett, Undercity) · 285–299 Chest – Major Stamina (Qia) · 299–300 Runed Arcanite Rod (Lorelae Wintersong). Note: the datamine gives Bracer – Inferior Stamina a learn skill of 20 and colours [0, 20, 22, 25], which conflicts with its 2–20 step.

**Forever changes:** Novice's Practice Wand and spell power on crafted wands; new weapon enchants Revelation, Recovery, Insight (140); necklace enchants (Strength, Agility, Spell Power, Healing Power, Deflection) at 210 for Merchant's Favor; off-hand enchants (Wisdom at 300 for Merchant's Favor; Superior Intellect, Excellent Spirit); new bracer enchants (Minor Intellect 70, Minor Healing Power 90, Lesser Agility 140, Agility 190, Lesser Healing Power 205); Mote of Magic (1 Strange Dust → 3 Motes); camp recipes Enchanted Lute (20), Arcane Salvager, Arcane Forge; Enchanting Certification for 1,000 Merchant's Favor; vendor prices of wands and oils changed several times during the beta.

## Profession overview

12 professions (9 primary, 3 secondary); up to 2 primary plus all secondary. SeeMeta (17 Oct 2026) counts 856 new recipes across professions; Zockify's counts differ. Campsite recipes: three per profession, the first at skill 20 from camping NPCs or trainers, later ones from dungeon bosses. Legacy talents Working Overtime and Dedicated Study help professions.
